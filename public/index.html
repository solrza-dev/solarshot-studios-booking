Step 2 — Your beat, your way

How will we handle your beat?

You'll answer this inside the booking form — but here's what each option means.

01

Send a beat link

Found a beat on YouTube, SoundCloud, or a type-beat page? Paste the link when you book and we'll have it loaded and ready.

02

Bring your own file

Got your own beat or instrumental? Upload it (or paste a Drive link) and we'll pull it up the moment you walk in.

03

Produce it live

No beat yet? Give us a reference sound or creative direction in the booking form and we'll build it in the room with you.

Before you book

📍 The studio

Session info and address are included with your booking confirmation. If it's your first time, don't stress — just show up with your energy and your files.

🎧 What to bring

Your beat link or file (or just a reference for the live production), any stems or acapellas you want to layer, and a list of the sounds you're chasing.

🗓️ Weekly schedule

Studio hours shift week to week. The calendar always reflects the current week's availability, so what you see is what's bookable.
